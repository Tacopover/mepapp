## EXACT SHAPES
```tsx
export interface ReconnectButtonProps {
  tooltip: string;
  disabled: boolean;
  /** Null: a click runs `onDirect` (no menu). */
  menu: ReconnectMenuItem[] | null;
  onChoose: (action: ReconnectMenuAction) => void;
  onDirect: () => void;
}
export function ReconnectButton(props: ReconnectButtonProps): JSX.Element;
```
- The trigger: `<button type="button" data-testid="reconnect-pdfs" aria-label="Reconnect PDFs" title={tooltip} disabled={disabled} aria-haspopup={menu ? 'menu' : undefined} aria-expanded={menu ? open : undefined}>Reconnect PDFs…</button>`.
- Menu (only when `menu !== null` and open): `<div role="menu" className=... data-testid="reconnect-menu">`; each item `<button role="menuitem" type="button" data-testid={item.testId} tabIndex={-1}>` with two lines: the label (strong) and the help (`span.mep-hint`). A click runs `onChoose(item.action)` and closes the menu.
- Keyboard (spec 6.2): Enter or Space on the trigger opens the menu and focuses item 1 (a normal button click does this: on open, focus item 1). ArrowDown and ArrowUp move between items (wrap). Enter or Space runs the item (native button). Esc closes and returns the focus to the trigger; `stopPropagation()` so the dialog does not close. Tab closes the menu (focus continues naturally). A `mousedown` outside closes it. The menu does not trap focus. The menu overlays (position absolute under the trigger, right aligned in the dialog at wide width, as wide as the band and left aligned under 900 px: `@media (max-width: 899px)` like the other narrow rules); it must not change the height of the band.
- Closing after a choice returns the focus to the trigger (the picker opens from the click).

## NEW BEHAVIOUR (what the dialog does now)
- PDFs go on a level only. With the building node selected there are no "Add PDFs…", "Add folder…", "Add to <level>" buttons and no per-document add buttons (`open-doc-add`, `open-doc-add-all-docs`). A hint `[data-testid="add-hint"]` says "Select a level to add PDFs." (or "Add a level first." in `[data-testid="open-documents"]` when there is no level). The refresh button `[data-testid="refresh-status"]` stays.
- With a level selected, "Add PDFs…" (button text `Add PDFs…`) and "Add folder…" place the pages on that level AT ONCE. There is no assign step and no `select[aria-label="Level for X page N"]`. A result line `[data-testid="add-result"]` says e.g. "Added 1 page to Level 1." (see `addResultText` in packages/ui/src/buildingSheets.ts) with a dismiss button `[data-testid="add-result-dismiss"]`. Pages already assigned: "N pages are already on Ground floor and were not moved." / "N pages are already on this level."
- The "Not placed" pane and tree node (`[role="treeitem"][data-kind="not-placed"]`, `[data-testid="sheets-pane"]`, `[data-testid="sheets-totals"]`, the "Not placed" ask on close/switch) stay only for old data and normally do not show. A page that was never assigned does not exist any more after an add.
- A PDF without a project id gets a MINTED id when added: the stored sheet has `projectUuid` (a uuid string) and `idMinted: true`; its status chip says "Not opened yet" (`data-status="not-opened"`) until the PDF is opened in the viewer; after Open the summary is written and the chip says "OK". A hand-built stored sheet with `projectUuid: null` (old data) still says "Not saved yet" with the hint "Not saved yet. Save it in MepApp to link it.".
- The open button of a sheet turns into a disabled "Opened" while its PDF is open in the viewer.
- Tree: levels are collapsed by default; a level has its own expand arrow and shows the PDF names under it. The "Open" badge next to a building was removed. A building can be renamed (Rename button). The open-documents list never shows an Untitled document without a PDF.
- Delete text under a level: "This level has N sheet(s). Move or remove it/them to delete the level."
- Model for style and for the fake picker: .claude/roadmap/work/b3-t13b-check.mjs (`putFile`, `pickList`, `window.showOpenFilePicker` replaced to honour `multiple`).

Step 3 "Amount", v2: need ÷ capacity

Mockup of the chosen concept. The user picks what they place. The kind of stamp gives a short list of needs. The room types give the numbers of the need, and the rule gives what one stamp gives (the capacity). The numbers calculate live. The rooms and the room-type numbers are examples (Dutch new build), not a real drawing.

View

Things that are the same in both layouts: the stamp (step 1) suggests the kind of stamp, and for sockets and data points also the capacity ("Double" = 2). Each kind of stamp has its defaults. The Limits section is closed until the user needs it. Without a capacity, the step shows a warning. Spacing, layout and Place (steps 4 to 6) do not change.

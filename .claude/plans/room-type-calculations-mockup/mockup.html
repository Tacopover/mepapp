Room types carry the calculations

Mockup for room-type-calculations.md. Each room type holds its needs, and each need has its calculation (per person, per m², 1 per N m², per room, air changes, illuminance). A room gets the calculations with its room type. The rule keeps the stamp side: what one stamp gives, limits, coverage and layout. Edits in a Room types card change step 3 at once. The numbers are examples (Dutch new build, room-demands-research.md §8), not a standard.

View

The same in all views: the four sample rooms are 1.20 Office (31.4 m², 5.8 m long), 1.05 Meeting room (18 m²), 1.01A Corridor (23.7 m², 13.2 m long) and 1.12 Toilet (3.2 m²), all 2.7 m high. The persons come from the room type: floor area ÷ area per person, rounded down (a number on the room itself still wins, as now). An empty room type has no needs: the "Fill in the example values" button of today fills them. Spacing, layout and Place (steps 4 to 6) do not change.
